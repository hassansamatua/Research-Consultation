## 🎨 **Design System:**

- **Info**: Yellow (`yellow-600`, `yellow-700`)
- **Neutral**: Gray shades (`gray-50` to `gray-900`)

### **Typography Scale**
- **Headers**: `text-lg font-semibold`
- **Titles**: `text-sm font-semibold`
- **Body**: `text-sm font-medium`
