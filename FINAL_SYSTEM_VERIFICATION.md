## 🎯 **Research Workflow Implemented**

1. **Registration** → Student creates account
2. **Supervisor Assignment** → Admin assigns supervisor
3. **Research Stages** → 7-stage progression:
   - Proposal
   - Chapter One (Introduction)
   - Chapter Two (Literature Review)
   - Chapter Three (Methodology)
   - Chapter Four (Results)
   - Chapter Five (Discussion)
   - Final Dissertation
4. **Document Submission** → Students upload at each stage
5. **Supervisor Review** → Feedback and approval
6. **Progress Tracking** → Real-time status updates
7. **Completion** → Final dissertation approval
